Fetch, fully simulated

Every force is real: gearmotor torque curves, rubber-on-court traction, weight transfer, and a center of gravity that climbs as 80 balls load the top hopper. The CG sits 3cm behind the drive axle because the sim proved it must, and the sloped nose deflector is there because the sim kept tripping the bot on its own lip until obstacle contact happened below the CG. Fill the hopper, switch the gyro limiter off, and drive it around. Known limit: a hard pile-up at the net or a fence can still wedge it - the wheels run on a scripted contact model for now, and full rigid-body wheel contact is the next iteration.
