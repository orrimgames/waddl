Waddl is a small robotics studio with one goal: a cute companion robot that anyone can afford. Our first bot is Pip - a duckling that waddles, flaps, and keeps you company at your desk.

Meet Pip Play with the sim

Why Waddl exists

Companion robots are the best category in consumer robotics, and they're priced like luxury goods. We think the cute robot pet should cost less than a video game console. So we're building one ourselves, in the open, one cheap servo at a time.

$40

Target build cost

Pip's prototype bill of materials starts around forty-five dollars, conversational tier under eighty-five. Four hobby servos, an ESP32, a battery, and a 3D-printed shell.

4

Servos, not 14

Servo count is what drives robot cost. Pip waddles and flaps on four SG90s. Charm is free; torque is not.

0

Cloud servers

Pip thinks and talks on-device. No account, no subscription, no server that can die. Your robot is yours forever. Every CAD file and build doc is open, too.

Roadmap

Where this goes, in order. No money moves until the plan earns it.

  1. Concept + sim - brand, CAD concept, and a playable web sim. you are here
  2. Prototype v0 - print the shell, wire 4 servos to an ESP32, first waddle on a real desk. Awaiting budget approval.
  3. Character pass - OLED eye animations, sounds, touch reactions. This is where Pip gets a personality.
  4. Kit or crowdfund - if people love the prototype, package it as a build-it-yourself kit.
